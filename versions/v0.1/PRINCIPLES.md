# COMASTA Principles

**Version 0.1 — Draft**

These principles define the current philosophical boundaries of COMASTA.

COMASTA is not designed to determine what a character must do.

It is designed to examine whether a proposed human action can be understood from the conditions surrounding that character, while preserving contradiction, deviation, and uncertainty.

---

## P01 — History Is Not Destiny

A character's history influences future behavior, but does not determine a single future action.

Past behavior should be treated as evidence for understanding a character, not as a deterministic rule for predicting that character.

**Principle:**

> History constrains and shapes possibility. It does not determine a single outcome.

---

## P02 — Plausibility Is Not Consistency

A behavior may be inconsistent with a character's established pattern while remaining humanly plausible.

Character consistency and human plausibility must therefore be evaluated separately.

A deviation from previous behavior is not automatically a narrative error.

---

## P03 — Contradiction Is Not Automatically an Error

Human beings may hold conflicting desires, values, emotions, and intentions at the same time.

A character may love someone and leave them.

A character may fear danger and still move toward it.

A character may resent someone and still care for them.

COMASTA should identify contradiction without automatically resolving or removing it.

---

## P04 — Multiple Outcomes May Remain Valid

The same character history and situation may support more than one plausible behavior.

COMASTA should not assume that sufficient contextual information necessarily produces one correct behavioral outcome.

The purpose of analysis is to examine a space of plausible actions, not to declare one inevitable action.

---

## P05 — Unknown Is a Valid State

Insufficient information is not a system failure.

When the available character information does not support a responsible assessment, COMASTA should be able to return:

`UNKNOWN`

or

`INSUFFICIENT CONTEXT`

rather than manufacture certainty.

---

## P06 — Triggers Modify Possibility, Not Destiny

Pressure, loss, urgency, relationships, environmental conditions, and other triggers may alter the plausibility of a behavior.

They do not determine the resulting behavior.

For example:

`HIGH PRESSURE`

does not mean:

`CHARACTER MUST ACT`

It means that behaviors which would normally represent significant deviation may become more understandable under the current conditions.

---

## P07 — Explanation Is Not Prediction

A system may be able to explain why an action is understandable after examining its conditions.

This does not mean the system could or should have predicted that the character would necessarily take that action.

COMASTA therefore distinguishes between:

- behavioral explanation
- behavioral plausibility
- behavioral prediction

These concepts must not be treated as equivalent.

---

## P08 — Uncertainty Should Not Be Eliminated by Default

More information may improve understanding without eliminating uncertainty.

The purpose of structure is not necessarily to produce certainty.

> The purpose of structure is to identify what can be understood — and where uncertainty must remain.

---

# 繁體中文

## COMASTA 基本原則

**版本 0.1 — 草案**

這些原則定義目前 COMASTA 的基本哲學邊界。

COMASTA 並不是用來決定一個角色「必須做什麼」。

它試圖檢視：在人物所處的生命條件之下，一項行為是否能夠被理解，同時保留人的矛盾、偏離與未知。

---

## P01 — 歷史不是命運

人物的歷史會影響未來行為，但不決定唯一的未來行動。

過去行為應作為理解人物的證據，而不是預測人物的決定性規則。

**原則：**

> 歷史會限制與塑造可能性，但不決定唯一結果。

---

## P02 — 成立不等於一致

一項行為可能不符合人物既有的行為模式，卻仍然在人性上成立。

因此，「角色一致性」與「人的行為是否成立」必須分開判斷。

人物偏離過去的自己，不應自動被視為敘事錯誤。

---

## P03 — 矛盾不必然是錯誤

人可以同時存在彼此衝突的慾望、價值、情緒與意圖。

一個人可以愛一個人，同時選擇離開。

一個人可以害怕危險，仍然走向危險。

一個人可以怨恨另一個人，同時仍然照顧他。

COMASTA 可以辨認矛盾，但不應自動消除或修正矛盾。

---

## P04 — 多種結果可以同時成立

相同的人物歷史與情境，可以支持不只一種合理的人類行為。

COMASTA 不應假設，只要取得足夠的人物資訊，就必然存在唯一正確的行為答案。

分析的目的，是檢視可能成立的行為空間，而不是宣布某個行為必然發生。

---

## P05 — 未知是合法狀態

資訊不足，不代表系統失敗。

當現有人物資料不足以支持負責任的判斷時，COMASTA 應允許輸出：

`UNKNOWN`

或：

`INSUFFICIENT CONTEXT`

而不是製造不存在的確定性。

---

## P06 — 觸發條件改變可能性，不決定命運

壓力、失去、迫切性、關係、環境等觸發條件，可以改變某項行為的成立程度。

但觸發條件不決定人物最後會採取哪個行動。

例如：

`HIGH PRESSURE`

不等於：

`CHARACTER MUST ACT`

它只表示：某些平常看似偏離人物既有模式的行為，在目前條件下可能變得更容易理解。

---

## P07 — 解釋不等於預測

系統可以根據人物條件，解釋為什麼某項行為可以成立。

但這不代表系統事前就能、或應該宣稱人物必然會採取這項行動。

因此 COMASTA 區分：

- 行為解釋
- 行為成立性
- 行為預測

三者不能被視為同一件事。

---

## P08 — 不確定性不應被預設消除

更多資訊可以增加理解，但不代表一定能消除未知。

結構存在的目的，不必然是得到更確定的答案。

> 結構的目的，是辨認我們能理解什麼，以及未知必須被保留在哪裡。

---
