# COMASTA Principles

**Version:** 0.2-draft
**Status:** Experimental Specification - Not Yet Validated

These principles define the philosophical boundary of COMASTA. The specification evaluates the interpretability of an externally supplied Candidate Action. It does not determine what a character must, will, or is most likely to do.

## P01 - History Is Not Destiny

> A character's history may shape future behavior without determining it.

History and prior behavior are evidence for interpretation. They are not deterministic instructions.

## P02 - Interpretability Is Not Prediction

Explaining why an action can be understood does not predict that the action will occur. No assessment state expresses probability, inevitability, optimality, or narrative preference.

## P03 - Deviation Is Descriptive, Not a Penalty

A Candidate Action may conflict with an established pattern while remaining interpretable. Baseline Conflict identifies that relationship; it does not subtract plausibility or function as a score.

## P04 - Evidence Must Be Grounded

Major assessment claims must be identified as `EXPLICIT`, `DERIVED`, or `UNGROUNDED`. Every `DERIVED` claim must identify the `EXPLICIT` claims on which it depends.

Speculative interpretations may be displayed separately. They must never function as supporting evidence, as a necessary Behavioral Bridge link, or as a reason to upgrade an assessment.

## P05 - Missing Evidence Is Not Negative Evidence

The absence of supporting information does not prove that an action is impossible. It may produce `UNSUPPORTED` when the current material contains no grounded explanatory path.

When a specific missing fact prevents responsible distinction between support and non-support, the assessment is `WITHHELD` with epistemic status `CRITICAL CONTEXT MISSING`.

## P06 - Contradiction Is Not Automatically an Error

Attachment and resentment, fear and approach, obligation and refusal, or other opposing conditions may remain simultaneously active. Contradiction must not be mathematically cancelled or automatically resolved into one true motive.

Unresolved Tensions must themselves be grounded in supplied information.

## P07 - Unknown Must Remain Open

Unknown information must not be silently completed. Open Unknowns are limited to missing information relevant to the current assessment target.

More information may improve interpretation without exhausting the character or producing a single correct future action.

## P08 - Multiple Actions May Remain Interpretable

The same history and situation may provide different grounded explanatory paths for different Candidate Actions. COMASTA does not rank those actions or identify the character's true response by default.

## P09 - Action and Expression Are Distinct Targets

An action may be interpretable while its exact dialogue, delivery, timing, or physical expression is not currently supported. When relevant, Action Interpretability and Expression Interpretability are assessed separately using the same grounding rules.

COMASTA v0.2-draft does not define a broad expression taxonomy or a separate divergence state.

## P10 - Authorial Authority Is Reserved

Authorial intention may define interpretive scope or protect ambiguity. It is not behavioral evidence and cannot upgrade an unsupported action.

COMASTA exposes what current material supports, derives, leaves unknown, or fails to ground. The author retains final authority and may deliberately choose an unsupported, ambiguous, or unexplained action.

## Core boundary

> COMASTA evaluates what can currently be understood from supplied material. It does not claim ownership of the character's future.
