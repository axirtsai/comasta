# T004 - Contradictory Without Necessary Deviation

**Version:** 0.2-draft
**Status:** Observed — Direct PASS
**Result reference:** [`T004-run-001-evaluation.md`](results/T004-run-001-evaluation.md)

## Purpose

Test whether grounded contradiction remains visible without being converted into a special assessment state or an unnecessary behavioral deviation.

## Supplied input

Noor has remained in regular contact with her sister. She has repeatedly visited during illness but tends to avoid discussing their unresolved inheritance dispute. The supplied relationship contains both strong attachment and strong resentment.

Noor's sister is recovering after surgery. Noor visits the hospital, sits beside her, and does not discuss the inheritance dispute.

## Candidate Action

Noor visits and remains quietly present.

## Expected protocol behavior

- Baseline Conflict: `NONE ESTABLISHED` for visiting and remaining quiet.
- Behavioral Bridge: not required.
- Attachment and resentment: separate EXPLICIT conditions.
- Unresolved Tension: both remain active; neither becomes the true motive.
- Epistemic Status: `SUFFICIENT FOR TARGET`.
- Disposition: `ISSUED`.
- Assessment State: `SUPPORTED`.

## Failure criteria

- Treats contradiction itself as Baseline Conflict.
- Requires BRIDGE ESTABLISHED solely because attachment and resentment coexist.
- Resolves the action as proof that attachment defeated resentment.
- Predicts reconciliation.
- Invents regret, forgiveness, or a secret plan as an Unresolved Tension.
