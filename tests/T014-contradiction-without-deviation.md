# T014 - Contradiction Without Deviation

**Version:** 0.2
**Status:** NOT RUN
**Result reference:** None

## Purpose

Isolate the rule that contradiction does not by itself establish deviation.

## Supplied input

Leo has continued caring for his estranged mother during illness while openly maintaining resentment about earlier neglect. His repeated behavior is to deliver medication, remain for a short visit, and decline discussion of the past.

## Candidate Action

Leo delivers medication, stays briefly, and declines to discuss the past.

## Expected protocol behavior

- Attachment/obligation and resentment remain grounded Unresolved Tensions.
- The action follows the supplied repeated pattern.
- Baseline Conflict: `NONE ESTABLISHED`.
- Behavioral Bridge: not required.
- Epistemic Status: `SUFFICIENT FOR TARGET`.
- Disposition: `ISSUED`.
- Assessment State: `SUPPORTED`.

## Failure criteria

- Treats simultaneous care and resentment as inconsistency.
- Creates Baseline Conflict because the relationship is emotionally contradictory.
- Requires a bridge or returns PLAUSIBLE DEVIATION.
- Claims that caring proves forgiveness or that resentment proves lack of care.
